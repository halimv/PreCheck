================================================================================
PROTOCOL SEQUENCE
================================================================================

STEP 1: Load Reagents
- Position 1: Stock solution tube (600 µL)
- Position 2: Diluent reservoir (5.5 mL minimum)
- Position 3: 96-well plate (empty, ready for dilutions)

STEP 2: Pre-Dilution Setup
- Aspirate 50 µL diluent into columns 2-12 (all 8 wells each)
- This provides the initial diluent in each well

STEP 3: Serial Transfer
- Transfer 50 µL from Column 1 (stock) → Column 2
- Transfer 50 µL from Column 2 → Column 3
- Transfer 50 µL from Column 3 → Column 4
- Continue through Column 12
- Mix after each transfer if required

STEP 4: Verification
- Final volume per well: 100 µL (50 µL original + 50 µL transfer)
- Document concentrations for downstream analysis

================================================================================
EXPECTED OUTCOMES
================================================================================

✓ 12 dilution points covering 4 log concentration range
✓ 2-fold dilution factor (each concentration = 50% of previous)
✓ 8 replicates per concentration (rows A-H)
✓ Linear decrease in concentration across columns
✓ Suitable for dose-response or titration experiments

================================================================================
NOTES & TROUBLESHOOTING
================================================================================

• Tip Changes: Use fresh tips for each transfer to prevent carryover
• Mixing: Consider adding mix steps after each transfer for better homogeneity
• Accuracy: Verify pipetting accuracy with positive control experiments
• Plate Orientation: Confirm plate orientation before running protocol
• Temperature: Maintain room temperature for most solutions (adjust if needed)
• Incubation: If required, add incubation steps between transfers
• Dead Volume: Account for ~20% dead volume in reservoirs and tubes
